Behind the scenes

How a cherry becomes a cup, frame by frame.

BLOOM is a student project for COMP5415 Multimedia Design and Authoring at the University of Sydney. This page shows how each part was made, and which parts were done by hand and which had AI help.

Tools used

Three tools, one little film.

BLOOM was built with three main programs. FreeMoCap was also tried for the wave, but the laptop could not process the recording, so it was not used.

  • Blender

    Modelling, animation and compositing

    The girl, the floating islands, every prop and every keyframe were made in Blender, along with the Bezier curves, Python scripts and Alpha Over compositing.

  • Figma

    Logo, posters and storyboard

    The BLOOM blossom logo, the three episode posters and the first-draft storyboard were designed in Figma.

  • Canva

    Editing, audio sync and subtitles

    Canva was used to edit the episodes, mix the narration with the music and effects, sync them to the picture and time every subtitle by hand. The presentation was finished in Canva too.

The pipeline

From sketch to screen in seven steps.

  1. Write the story Own work

    Anugraha wrote the three-episode story and the narration script, gathered a mood board of reference images and designed the first-draft storyboard in Figma.

  2. Model and animate AI-guided

    Each 960-frame scene was built and keyframed in Blender, following step-by-step instructions from ChatGPT. Bezier curves and Python scripts drive the milk stream, the coffee waterfall and the girl's limb poses, and ChatGPT helped fix overlaps and errors.

  3. Keyframe the wave Own work

    A FreeMoCap motion-capture attempt kept crashing the laptop, so Anugraha keyframed the girl's wave by hand in Blender's Animation workspace and Graph Editor.

  4. Composite the layers AI-guided

    In Scene 1 the girl and the scenery render as separate layers, joined by two Alpha Over nodes in the Blender compositor. ChatGPT gave instructions for the setup.

  5. Voice and sound AI-assisted

    The narration is Anugraha's own voice, pitch-shifted with ChatGPT to sound like a storyteller. The music and effects, fetched using ChatGPT, were synthesised with Python/NumPy, not stock recordings.

  6. Edit, caption and publish Own work

    In Canva, Anugraha synced the narration, music and effects to the picture and timed every subtitle by hand. The three episodes were exported as MP4 for this site.

  7. Build and publish the website AI-assisted

    The website was enhanced with Claude through Claude Code, which also assisted with its animations: the floating cup, steam and pour, the drifting clouds, the scroll reveals, the click bee and the episode crossfade. Claude then pushed the code to GitHub and deployed the site on GitHub Pages, so it can be opened from a link.

Posters

One poster per episode.

Made in Figma from Anugraha's own Blender renders. Each poster shares the same dark frame, BLOOM wordmark and journey tracker (harvest, roast and grind, brew), so the three read as one series, and each episode gets its own accent colour: cherry red, roast gold and crema cream.
